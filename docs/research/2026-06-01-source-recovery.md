## What the repo brief establishes

The local June 1 brief recommends a path-first guided experience instead of presenting the Campaign Agent's six broad collection fields. It proposes:

- choosing a use case before asking three to four focused setup questions;
- starting enrichment as soon as a company, content, or event URL is entered;
- inferring brand, product, messaging, template, and audience context in the background;
- returning a fast shareable preview with accept, regenerate, audience, and brand adjustments;
- keeping additional information and generation instructions optional rather than blocking.

The June 1 artifact is historical product evidence. July 30 V1 decisions in this repository govern where they differ, including folding event behavior into the generalized campaign path and using the three current V1 use cases.
